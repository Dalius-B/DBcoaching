Privacy

What happens to what you tell me.

There is no clever version of this page. You fill in an application, it reaches Dalius, and nothing else happens to it.

Last updated 1 September 2026

Who is responsible

DB Coaching, run by Dalius Butkus. If you want anything on this page acted on, message @dalius_butkus on Instagram, or reply to any email you have already received about your application.

Where the application form lives

The application form is a Google Form. Clicking any apply button on this site opens it in a new tab on Google's own domain, and nothing you type into it passes through this site at all. This page collects nothing.

What you fill in there is held in Google's systems and reaches Dalius through his Google account. Google's own privacy terms cover that step, and its handling of your data is not something this site controls.

Some of what the form asks describes your health and fitness. You are never obliged to write more than you want to.

What the application form collects

Only what is typed into it. Nothing is gathered in the background, and there is no hidden field.

The fields

  • You: name, email, which option you are applying for, age, height and weight.
  • Your goal: what you want, and why it matters to you now.
  • Your training history: experience level, whether you have followed a program before, what has and has not worked.
  • Your schedule: days a week, session length, and where you train.
  • Injuries and health: current injuries or pain, past injuries, movements that hurt, and any medical conditions or medication.
  • Your life and food: your job, hours of sleep, stress level, daily steps, dietary restrictions and where you struggle with food.
  • Working together: what you expect from a coach, whether you are willing to send weekly weight updates and progress photos, and how you found the site.

A good part of that is health information: injuries, medication, weight, sleep and stress. Under the GDPR that kind of detail needs your explicit permission rather than an assumption, which is what the consent box at the end of the form is for. Nothing is compulsory beyond what the form marks as required, and where a question does not apply you can simply say so.

Agreeing to send weekly weight updates or progress photos is a question, not a requirement. Answering no does not stop an application, and no photo is ever requested through this website.

What it is used for

Reading your application, deciding whether the coaching is a fit, and getting back to you. It is not used to build a mailing list, it is not used for advertising, and it is not shared or sold to anyone.

Who else handles it

These services are involved in this site working at all. None of them are given your application to do anything else with.

  • Google Forms hosts the application itself and stores the answers, on Google's terms rather than these.
  • Web3Forms receives the form and forwards it as an email. It is the postbox between the page and the inbox.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web server it records requests, including IP addresses, in its own logs.
  • Google Fonts serves the two typefaces the page uses. Loading them means your browser contacts Google and, unavoidably, reveals your IP address in doing so.

What this site does not do

Worth stating plainly, because most sites cannot.

Not happening here

  • No cookies. The site sets none at all, which is why there is no cookie banner.
  • No analytics, no tracking pixels, no session recording.
  • No advertising, and no third-party ad or marketing scripts.
  • Nothing stored in your browser. No local storage, no session storage.
  • No account, no password, no profile.

How long it is kept

Your application is kept while it is being considered, and while we are working together if you go ahead. If you do not go ahead, it stays in the form responses and the inbox until you ask for it to be removed. Ask, and it is deleted.

What you can ask for

If you are in the EU or the UK, the law gives you these rights. In practice they are honoured for everyone, because the amount of information involved is small enough that there is no reason not to.

  • A copy of what was submitted.
  • A correction if something is wrong.
  • Deletion, at any time and without giving a reason.
  • Withdrawal of consent, which stops any further contact.
  • A complaint to your national data protection authority if the answer you get is not good enough.

Ask through the Instagram account above, or by replying to any email already exchanged, and it will be dealt with.

If this page changes

The date at the top changes with it. There is no separate archive, but the full history of this page is public in the repository the site is built from, so any edit can be seen and compared.
